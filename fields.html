Our Field Locations

All league games and weekly practice drills are hosted at local sports complexes within the Borough of Fair Haven, New Jersey. Please check your weekend schedule closely to confirm your team's designated pitch allocation.

Fair Haven Fields Complex

Address: Ridge Road, Fair Haven, NJ 07704

Our primary expansive complex featuring multiple designated layout options tailored for both small-sided youth matches and full-sized 11v11 configurations for older divisions. Ample public parking is accessible along the main Ridge Road entrance hub.

Weather Delays & Field Cancellations: Field usage decisions are carefully coordinated with the field maintenance crews. In cases of torrential rains or unsafe playing surfaces, field status updates will be blasted out via email before 7:30 AM on match Saturdays.
