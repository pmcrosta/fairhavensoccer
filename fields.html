Our Field Locations

All K-2 and 3-8 home league games and weekly practice drills are hosted at Fair Haven Fields. Please check your schedule closely to confirm your team's designated pitch allocation.

Fair Haven Fields Complex

Address: Dartmouth Rd, Fair Haven, NJ 07704

Our primary complex featuring multiple field configurations tailored for youth matches and older division match play. Ample public parking is accessible along the main Ridge Road entrance layout.

Note for K-2 Divisions: The Kindergarten through 2nd Grade program splits play across 9 small fields at this complex, each clearly marked with highly visible signage numbers 1 through 9. Your layout could change weekly; verify your current Field location assignment via TeamSnap before your Saturday slot.

Weather Delays & Field Cancellations: Field usage decisions are carefully coordinated with the field maintenance crews. In cases of torrential rains or unsafe playing surfaces, field status updates will be blasted out via email and TeamSnap before 7:30 AM on match Saturdays.
