3. Typography

  • Wordmark & Display Headings: Classic serif (Noto Serif, Georgia, or Times New Roman) set in uppercase with slight letter-spacing for the main "FAIR HAVEN SOCCER" banner, and title case for section headers.
  • Body & UI Copy: Clean system sans-serif (-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Arial, sans-serif) in Dark Slate (#223345) for high legibility on mobile and desktop screens.
  • Jersey Back Sponsorships: Clean, bold sans-serif block lettering in contrasting single color (White on dark jerseys; Navy/Black on light jerseys).
