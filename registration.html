Fair Haven Soccer

Fall 2026 Registration Information

Registration Window: Registration is open from June 15th through August 2nd. A $40 late fee applies after August 2nd, and space on rosters is no longer guaranteed.

Open Player Registration Form

Season Timeline

Who: Fair Haven residents entering K–8th Grade (Fall 2026).

Dates: The Fall 2026 soccer season is scheduled to begin on September 12th and run through November 7th, 2026.

Registration Cost & Fees

  • Grades K-2: $105 per player
  • Grades 3-8: $115 per player
  • Late Fee: +$40 (Applies to all registrations submitted after August 2nd)

Discounts & Assistance

  • Sibling Discount: A discount is automatically applied when you register multiple children across the same account.
  • Fee Reductions: Available for active military members and our dedicated township staff, including but not limited to Fair Haven Borough employees, the Fair Haven Fire/First Aid Department, and the Fair Haven Police Department.
  • Financial Assistance: We are strictly committed to ensuring all children can play. If your family requires financial assistance, please reach out to us at fhnjrecsoccer@gmail.com to arrange confidential support.

Business Sponsorships

Support youth sports in our community! A corporate or small business sponsorship of $200 per team will get your business name cleanly printed on the back of a team jersey for the entire Fall season.
