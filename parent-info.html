Parent Information & Season Notes

Welcome to the season! Please review these important guidelines and scheduling details to help ensure a smooth, fun experience for all of our players and volunteer coaches.

Team Communication via TeamSnap ONE

Please use TeamSnap ONE to send messages to your team and update your child's 'Status' if they will attend that weekend's session (Yes/No/Maybe). You can also find contact information for team members by clicking into a child's name on the roster.

NOTE that you do not need to sign up for any TeamSnap subscriptions in order to participate in the season. Feel free to skip or press cancel!

Action Item: Please do your best to update your child's availability consistently. It gives our volunteer parent coaches a good idea of what to expect each week so they can plan practice drills and scrimmages effectively.

Mandatory Equipment & Apparel

  • Shin Guards: Absolutely required for every session. Please note that shin guards must go under the soccer socks.
  • Cleats: It is highly recommended that players wear proper soccer cleats to maintain traction and avoid injury.
  • Water: Please bring plenty of water for your child to stay hydrated. Additional drinks and snacks can be purchased at the Concession Stand after each session.
  • Soccer Balls (K-2 only): Please purchase and have your child bring exactly 1 ball to training every week:
    • Kindergarten & 1st Grade: Size 3 ball
    • 2nd Grade: Size 4 ball
    • Please do not bring a size 5 ball for K-2. We highly recommend writing your child's or family's name clearly on the ball with a permanent marker.

Uniform Distribution

(For K-2) Each player will receive a league T-shirt and soccer socks, which are handed out on Saturday at the first session of the season. Please arrive 10-15 minutes early to your first session to pick up your child's shirt and socks at the table located near the playground. Parents are responsible for outfitting their child in their own soccer shorts.

(For 3-8) Each player will receive a kit consisting of shirt, shorts, and soccer socks. Your coach will distribute at the first practice or game.

K-2 Format

All of our K-2 cohorts will receive complete schedule uploads inside TeamSnap ONE. At each 1-hour session, the first 30 minutes will be spent on skills and development activities, and the final 30 minutes are dedicated to a scrimmage against another team.

Field Layouts: The K-2 program utilizes 9 distinct fields at Fair Haven Fields that will be clearly marked with visible signs. Your home field designation could change each week, so please check TeamSnap ONE ahead of time. In the schedule, you will see each week listed as “Team Name 1 vs. Team Name 2”. The "Location" section of that specific date will highlight which Field # your team will start out on with their coaches.

Scrimmage Execution: In most cases, each team will divide in half. Half of Team 1 will play against Half of Team 2 on Field A, while the remaining players scrimmage on the other half's assigned field space. This setup allows for optimal, small-sided games (~ 5 v 5) and creates a highly interactive, fun experience playing against other teams. The coaches will help coordinate all of this on the ground.

3-8 Format

Our 3rd through 8th graders play in the Northern Monmouth Soccer Association. Home games are at Fair Haven Fields, and away games are in the participating towns of Eatontown, Little Silver, Long Branch, Red Bank, Rumson, Shore (Monmouth Beach, Oceanport, West Long Branch), Shrewsbury, and Tinton Falls

Game play:3rd/4th play 7v7, 5th/6th play 9v9, and 7th/8th play 11v11.
