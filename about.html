Fair Haven Soccer

About Our Organization

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide a community based soccer program that is safe, enjoyable, and competitive while supporting player development, and fostering a sense of belonging.

Our soccer program is entirely supported by a community of volunteers which give their time and effort to ensure our children have an opportunity to participate, learn, develop, and enjoy the game of soccer.

Our Core Philosophy

Fair Haven Rec Soccer is a township-focused community athletic program dedicated entirely to children residing in Fair Haven, New Jersey. The program stands as a vibrant entry point for youth players to discover the fundamentals of the sport, build friendships, and learn the value of teamwork.

Our guiding objective is to curate a highly positive, encouraging, and balanced environment where soccer instruction is made accessible and non-threatening. There are no tryouts required—every registered resident child is guaranteed placement on a team with balanced, equitable playtime across matches.

Volunteer Board & Leadership

The program is structured and sustained through our community board members and parent volunteers working in alignment with Fair Haven's Parks and Recreation department guidelines. Board meetings are regularly synchronized to optimize local community recreation standards.
