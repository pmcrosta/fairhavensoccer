Coach & Volunteer Information

We always need volunteer coaches! Our soccer program is entirely supported by a community of volunteers who give their time and effort to ensure our children have an opportunity to participate, learn, develop, and enjoy the game of soccer.

Mandatory Certification Rules: New Jersey state law and local township bylaws mandate that every volunteer coach completes official safety instruction and undergoes a background check prior to coaching.

1. Rutgers S.A.F.E.T.Y. Clinic

All volunteer coaches are required to attend and successfully complete the Sports Awareness For Educating Today's Youth (S.A.F.E.T.Y.) clinic. This foundational course is mandatory for all volunteers on the field.

To locate upcoming classes and secure enrollment instructions, review the official portal. We recommend the online class: Rutgers Youth Sports Research Council.

2. Required Background Checks

All volunteer coaches will be required to undergo background checks. Fair Haven Rec Soccer coordinates this with the Borough. Background checks are required every 2 years.

What Volunteers Can Expect

  • Time Commitment: For Grades K-2, your main commitment is the Saturday session. For Grades 3-8, expect one weekday team practice session alongside the standard Saturday match program.
  • Equipment Provided: For 3-8, the league provides safety and first-aid equipment, practice equipment, cones, and match soccer balls to our head coaches at the beginning of the season. For K-2, safety equipment is located on a folding table near the K-2 fields.

Coaching Resources & Practice Toolkits

We want to ensure our volunteers feel confident on the pitch. Use the session plans, playbooks, and league rules below to run engaging, age-appropriate practices throughout the season:

  • Club Training Guide: FH Rec Soccer Coach Activities (PDF) — Our handbook of warm-ups, fun drills, and field setup diagrams.
  • 3rd to 8th Grade League Rules (NMSA): Official NMSA Rules 2026 (PDF) — Match guidelines, field dimensions, game lengths, substitution protocols, and referee rules for 3rd–8th Grade inter-town play.
  • Grades K–2 (U6–U8) Playbooks & Games: Simple scrambling games, tag activities, and introduction to ball mastery.
    • Mass Youth Soccer U6 & U8 Session Plans — Weekly printable 45–60 min plans.
    • Indiana Soccer 10-Week Grassroots Curriculum — Structured weekly practice outlines.
    • SoccerXpert U6/U8 Drills — Fun favorites like Sharks & Minnows and Clean Your Room.
  • Grades 3–5 (U9–U11) Playbooks & Drills: Passing shapes, spatial awareness, defending basics, and goalkeeper fundamentals.
    • Mass Youth Soccer U10 & U12 Session Plans — Passing triangles and transition play.
    • Indiana Soccer U10–U12 Practice Plans — Combination play and small-sided games.
    • SoccerXpert U10/U12 Small-Sided Drills — 3v2, 4v3, and finishing exercises.
  • Grades 6–8 (U12–U14) Tactical Guides: Match positioning strategy, team defense, and transition drills.
    • Mass Youth Soccer U14 Tactical Plans — Zonal defending, pressing, and restarts.
    • SoccerXpert U14/U16 Tactical Drills — Spacing, midfield distribution, and team defense.
  • Coaching Education & Licensing:
    • U.S. Soccer Learning Center — Free 20-minute online introductory Grassroots coaching module.
    • NJ Youth Soccer Coaching Education — State clinic schedules and Grassroots licensing pathways.
